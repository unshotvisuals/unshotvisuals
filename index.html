07 / FAQ
Good questions.

    

      
What do I get with the Test Shot?
3 campaign images, 3 story versions and 3 ad creatives, starring someone from our cast. Think of it as a first date. Book a package after, and the full amount comes off.

      
Will it look like AI?
That's kind of the whole point: it won't. Real light, real shadows, real skin, real fabric. If something looks off, we fix it before you ever see it.

      
What is an exclusive character?
A face built from scratch for your brand. You approve it, and it's never used for anyone else. It takes serious work, so it comes with The Signature, Premium and Always On.

      
What do I need to send?
Clear product photos, your logo, and anything that shows your taste. The form walks you through it. No homework, promise.

      
Do I only get images?
Nope. Depending on your package: story versions, ad creatives, motion reels, edited layouts and templates. It lands ready to post.

      
How long does it take?
Test Shot: 2 to 3 days. Bigger campaigns: about a week after you sign off on the idea. Still faster than booking a studio.

      
Will my logo and label be exact?
Yes. Logos and small text go in by hand, so your branding is always sharp. No melted letters here.

      
How do I pay?
50% to start, 50% on delivery. EGP or USD, same price.
